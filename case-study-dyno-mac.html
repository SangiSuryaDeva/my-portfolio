Dyno-Mac

An internal suggestion portal accessed through the client’s HRMS. The original process used paper suggestions; the client wanted a digital workflow to review ideas, make decisions, and track approved projects.

Starting from a verbal brief

The client explained the existing physical suggestion-box process verbally, without a detailed requirements document. I translated the brief into the application structure and designed the product from scratch. The design was frozen in the first round.

Employee: submit an idea with context

The submission form captures a brief explanation, budget estimate, expected duration if approved, company benefit, and target. These fields help reviewers understand the idea and its potential implications.

Manager: review constructively

Managers can reject a suggestion with a reason, approve it with a 1–5 star rating and explanation, or partially approve it with specific modification feedback. Partial approval returns the suggestion to the employee without treating it as a rejection.

Top Management: decide and prioritize

Top Management receives manager-approved suggestions, with higher-rated ideas prioritized. It can approve or reject an idea, and rejection requires a reason—even when a manager previously rated it highly.

Track approved projects

The Top Management dashboard organizes suggestions by rating and status, including approved, in progress, completed, in process, and failed. Approved projects can track timelines, spent and remaining budget, daily progress updates, and photos of key updates.

The key design decision

Separating partial approval from rejection created a constructive modification loop. Instead of ending the process when an idea needs work, the interface makes the next step and feedback explicit.

Outcome

A complete digital suggestion-management workflow designed from scratch for an internal product accessed through HRMS. The design was frozen in the first round. No organizational improvement percentage is claimed without verified evidence.

Product architecture, role-based workflows, forms, decision states, dashboards, feedback loops, project tracking, and end-to-end UI design.

Project context

Overview & design challenge.

01 / Overview

An internal suggestion portal accessed through the client HRMS, replacing a physical suggestion box and paper-based review with a trackable digital workflow.

02 / Problem to solve

Employees needed to submit ideas with enough context; managers needed to respond constructively; top management needed prioritized decisions and project tracking. There was no formal requirements document, so the experience was structured from a verbal brief.

Design rationale

Key design decisions.

Grounded in the project details available for this portfolio.

Capture decision-ready context

The form includes the idea, expected company benefit, target, estimated budget, and duration if approved.

Make partial approval distinct

Managers can request modifications with specific feedback instead of forcing every suggestion into approve/reject.

Connect approval to execution

Approved ideas can be tracked with timeline, budget spent/remaining, progress updates, and photos.

Outcome & learning

What this work reinforced

A useful internal product does more than collect submissions: it helps people evaluate, respond, act, and understand what happens next.
