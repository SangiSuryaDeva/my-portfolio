Case study overview

SSR Dailli

A live dairy commerce and distribution ecosystem across four experiences: a customer app, a delivery-agent app, a distributor app, and a central web application for receiving and routing orders.

Four connected experiences

The customer-facing SSR Dailli app lets customers book dairy products, including recurring monthly milk bookings for daily doorstep delivery. The SSR Delivery app supports area-based assignments, pickup and delivery locations, and OTP confirmation at pickup and delivery. The SSR Distributors app supports ordering quantities based on demand, tracking amounts paid and outstanding, crates supplied, and empty crates held. A central web application receives orders and sends them to distributors.

Customer app: recurring demand

The recurring booking experience lets a customer arrange milk for a month, with daily delivery. This model creates a different need from a one-time cart checkout and must be easy to understand.

Delivery app: operational confirmation

Delivery agents are assigned areas and can see where products should be picked up and where they need to be delivered. OTP confirmation at pickup and at the destination is part of the described flow.

Distributor app: orders and accountability

Distributors order quantities based on demand and need visibility into payments, outstanding amounts, crates supplied, and empty crates held. These records are central to the distributor experience.

Central web application

The web application receives orders and routes them to distributors, connecting customer demand with the distribution side of the product.
