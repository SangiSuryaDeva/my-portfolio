About me

Curiosity, structure,
and purpose.

I’m Surya Deva Sangi, a Senior UI/UX & Product Designer with 5+ years of experience shaping enterprise applications, healthcare and AI experiences, B2B/B2C platforms, dashboards, and marketplaces.

My point of view

I design beyond the screen.

A good interface is the visible part of a much larger system.

I look at the people using a product, the work they need to complete, the information they rely on, the business rules behind each action, and the technical constraints that shape delivery. The goal is not simply to make a screen look good—it is to make the whole experience feel clear and dependable.

Whether the product supports a clinician, warehouse team, service customer, or internal decision-maker, I aim to reduce unnecessary effort and make the next step easier to understand.

“Good products happen when business goals, technology, and human needs come together thoughtfully.”
— Surya Deva Sangi
